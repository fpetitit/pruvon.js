# Simple Arithmetic (Markdown)

Same spec as [basket.pruvon.html](basket.pruvon.html), written in Markdown to
demonstrate the `pruvon:<fnName>` fence convention.

## Sum

```pruvon:sum
| Operand 1 | Operand 2 | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | 1 |
| 5 | 3 | 8 |
```

## Sub

```pruvon:sub
| Operand 1 | Operand 2 | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 1 | 1 |
| 4 | 5 | -1 |
```
